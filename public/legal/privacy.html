Privacy & Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ebenezer Higher Secondary School, Sabroom (S), Tripura (“the School”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, stores and protects personal information through the School App and website (“the App”). The App is used by students, parents/guardians, teachers, class teachers, the principal and administrative staff. We process personal data in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India) and other applicable laws.

1. Information we hold

  • Student records: name, admission number, class, section, roll number, date of birth, gender, blood group, house group, residential address, PIN code, contact number, parents’ names, transport mode, hosteler/day-scholar status and photograph.
  • Staff records: name, date of birth, gender, email, phone number, address, PIN code, blood group, date of joining, qualifications, subject/department, class assigned and photograph.
  • Login data: username / admission number and a protected (hashed) password. Student passwords are their date of birth, which is already part of the school record.
  • School Group (chat) content: text messages, pictures and videos you send.
  • Technical data: a temporary security cookie that keeps you signed in, and basic server logs (date, time, IP address) used only to detect misuse.

2. Why we use it

  • To let you sign in securely and show you your own dashboard and profile.
  • To share notices, announcements, calendars, results, attendance and fee information.
  • To provide the School Group chat between students, teachers and school management.
  • To keep the App safe, prevent misuse and meet our legal duties as a school.

We do not sell, rent or use your data for advertising, and we do not show ads in the App.

3. Who can see your information

  • Each student can see only their own profile.
  • Class teachers, the principal, administrative staff and the App Administrator can see records needed for their school duties.
  • School Group monitoring: for the safety of children, the App Administrator can view messages inside groups created in the chat-room. Please use the chat responsibly.
  • We share data outside the School only when required by law or by a lawful order of an authority.

4. Chat messages are deleted automatically after 60 days

Every message, picture and video in the School Group is stored temporarily and is permanently deleted at midnight (12:00 AM) after 60 days from the day it was sent. Each message has its own timer — for example, a message sent on 01-10-2026 is removed at midnight on 30-11-2026. Deleted chat content cannot be recovered.

Chat content is encrypted while it is transmitted and stored on the School’s server.

5. How we protect your data

  • Passwords are stored only in hashed (scrambled) form — nobody, including staff, can read them.
  • Every page and file is checked on the server so each person can open only what their role allows.
  • Repeated wrong logins temporarily lock the account to stop guessing attacks.
  • The Admin Panel runs separately from the main App with its own extra protection.
  • Photos and records are never made publicly available on the internet.

6. How long we keep data

School records are kept for as long as the student or staff member is with the School and afterwards for the period required by education and legal rules. Chat content is kept for a maximum of 60 days. Sign-in cookies expire when you log out or after a period of inactivity.

7. Children’s privacy

Many users are below 18 years. Student accounts are created only by the School with the knowledge of parents/guardians at the time of admission. Parents/guardians may contact the School to review or correct their child’s information.

8. Your rights

You may ask the School to access, correct or update your personal data, or raise a concern about how it is handled. Requests can be made in writing at the school office.

9. Cookies

The App uses one essential security cookie to keep you signed in. It cannot be read by other websites and is removed when you log out. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available in the App.
